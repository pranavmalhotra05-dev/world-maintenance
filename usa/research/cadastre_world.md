# World cadastre: open parcel boundary licences

Audit date 2026-11-25. Green means an explicit licence allows commercial use and redistribution (public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, ODbL, OGL, Licence Ouverte, dl-de, NLOD, or terms that say so). Grey means disclaimer-only, unstated, conditional or not verified. Red means paid, non-commercial, view-only or no bulk/redistribution. Data: `cadastre_world.json` in this folder.

Caveats: the shared web-search budget ran out part-way, so several rows rest on catalogue licence fields (GovData, data.gov.lv, podatki.gov.si, data.gov.hr, dados.gov.pt, data.gov.ie) rather than a licence sentence, and some pages blocked the fetcher (HMLR, RoS, opendata.swiss, mass.gov, several Australian portals). Those are flagged in notes. Nothing was downloaded beyond GetCapabilities/metadata.
