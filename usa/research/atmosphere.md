# Atmosphere lane: in-situ and ground-based weather data (closer than satellites)

Researched 2026-11-26 with the built-in browser; licences read on official pages, key endpoints probed with curl (200 OK noted in JSON). Full records incl. "what we could show": `atmosphere.json`.

Verdict rule: GREEN only for public domain / CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-SA / ODbL / explicit commercial OK and permanently free. Unstated or account-gated restrictions = GREY. Non-commercial, paid or no-redistribution = RED.

## Top 5 recommendations

1. **NWS Local Storm Reports via IEM (GREEN, not in app).** `mesonet.agron.iastate.edu/geojson/lsr.geojson` - no key, public domain. Richer than SPC: includes sub-severe hail, measured gusts and "tree down on house / power lines" remarks. Show spotter pins and flag houses within ~2 km of hail >= 1 in or tree damage. Quickest win for roof + tree leads.
2. **CoCoRaHS hail pads and rain gauges (GREEN, CC BY 4.0, not in app).** `data.cocorahs.org/export/exportreports.aspx?ReportType=Hail&Format=CSV` - no key; fields include measured stone size and damage text. Volunteers are densest in suburbs, where the houses are. Ground-truths MRMS MESH and gives street-level rain totals for gutter/overflow leads. Credit "CoCoRaHS" on display.
3. **More MRMS products on the existing pipeline (GREEN; MESH already in app).** Add `RotationTrack` (tornado/strong-wind swaths) and `MultiSensor_QPE_24H/72H` from `s3://noaa-mrms-pds`, reusing the per-house MESH sampler.
4. **HRRR forecast gusts / updraft helicity (GREEN, catalog ready).** `s3://noaa-hrrr-bdp-pds`, no account. "Storm likely over these blocks tomorrow" for crew pre-positioning; label as forecast, never as observed.
5. **NEXRAD Level II 3D storm replay (GREEN).** `s3://unidata-nexrad-level2` (new bucket since Sep 2025). The storm's reflectivity as a fine glowing dot cloud above the block, matching the dot-globe style. Secondary visual: SondeHub balloon tracks (CC BY-SA 2.0; share-alike applies to derived data).

## Notable exclusions
- RED: Synoptic Data (commercial is paid; free tier non-commercial), Oklahoma Mesonet (copyrighted, no redistribution, commercial fees; other state mesonets need checking one by one), Vaisala NLDN (paid), Blitzortung (non-commercial), PurpleAir (custom ToS, grantback, paid points), AAAAI pollen (all rights reserved).
- GREY: MADIS (account; mesonet tier bars web redistribution), University of Wyoming soundings (no terms stated; use IGRA instead), AirNow real-time, OpenAQ (per-provider), wind profilers (MADIS route).
- GREEN but low lead value: IGRA/NWS soundings, ozonesondes, GML towers, AmeriFlux (CC BY sites; account needed), USCRN, hurricane recon/dropsondes (landfalls only).
- No green US pollen network or green ground-based lightning network found; GOES GLM (in app) stays the only green lightning source.
